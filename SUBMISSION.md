# OVERTYPE — Orynth Game Cup submission copy

## Name
OVERTYPE

## Engine / Framework
Vanilla JS / Canvas

## One-line hook
The faster you type, the slower they come.

(Alternates: "Out-type the swarm before it overruns you." / "A typing survivor where speed is the weapon.")

## What's your game about? (under 500 chars)
OVERTYPE is a neon typing survival game. You're a lone core on a synthwave grid, and words rush in from every edge. Type a word to destroy it. The twist is Flow: type fast and clean and you fill an Overdrive meter that slams the whole swarm into slow motion while your score doubles. It's a typing test that fights back, built for that flow-state rush where your fingers outrun your brain. Level up, grab an upgrade, and see how long you last before you're overrun.

## How do you play? (under 600 chars)
Survive. Words drift in from all sides toward your core in the center; type one to destroy that enemy before it reaches you. Just start typing and you lock onto a matching word, and the lock follows your letters, so typing "cou" slides you from "cow" to "couch". First 30 seconds: clear the closest words, keep a clean combo going, and watch the ring around your core fill. Fill it for Overdrive, where the swarm crawls and points double. Every several words you level up and type a short word to pick an upgrade.

## Controls
Just type. Letter keys type the words on incoming enemies, and the lock follows your prefix so you can switch targets mid-word. On level up, type the short word shown on a card to pick that upgrade. Press Enter to restart after a loss.

## What makes it fun?
The Flow and Overdrive loop. Typing fast and clean fills a meter that drops the entire swarm into slow motion and doubles your score, so raw typing speed becomes a superpower instead of just a stat. Flexible prefix targeting means you never fumble for a word; you keep typing and the lock follows, which holds you in an unbroken flow state.

## Notable tools & assets
Plain HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript, no game engine or libraries. Every visual is drawn in code (no art assets) and every sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API. The whole game is one self-contained HTML file. Built with AI assistance (Claude / Claude Code).

## Browser compatibility
Runs in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (modern desktop browsers). Mobile is not supported because the game needs a physical keyboard, so leave "also works on mobile" unchecked. It is fully playable in the browser with no downloads, installs, launchers, or extensions, so check the confirmation box.
